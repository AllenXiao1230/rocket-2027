# Require Final-Configuration Fault-Injection Evidence

Qualification for the shared flight-intent avionics must separately demonstrate Main and Backup functions in the final shared enclosure and include controlled peer, power-path and RF single-fault scenarios. Normal end-to-end operation alone is insufficient evidence that the independent fault domains and authority transfer work.

## Consequences

The exact faults, thresholds and pass criteria remain validation-gated TBDs, but the evidence method is fixed before actuator selection or a 2027 LRR checklist is available.
