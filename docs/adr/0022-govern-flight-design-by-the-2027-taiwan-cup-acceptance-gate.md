# Govern flight design by the 2027 Taiwan Cup acceptance gate

This rocket is being designed for the 2027 Taiwan Cup, so final flight configuration is governed by that year's official competition rules, LRR and written RF conditions, together with NCC applicability and flight-test evidence. As of 2026-08-25 no public 2027 rule set was found; 2026 material is a conservative planning baseline only, not a claim of 2027 approval.

## Consequences

The dual-flight-computer architecture, recovery independence and evidence-first testing remain the target design. RF waveform, power, airtime, antenna configuration and any other item subject to the 2027 documents remain configurable until the Competition Acceptance Gate is satisfied; no unverified allowance becomes a flight assumption.
